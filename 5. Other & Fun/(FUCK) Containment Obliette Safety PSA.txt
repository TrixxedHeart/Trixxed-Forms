[head=3]PURPOSE & FUNCTION[/head]
The Containment Oubliette is a specialized decontamination device designed to rid individuals of anomalous contaminants, memetic hazards, and reality-altering afflictions. When properly utilized, it safely neutralizes these threats without permanent harm to the subject.

[head=3]CRITICAL WARNINGS[/head]
[color=red][bold]MISUSE USE MAY RESULT IN CATASTROPHIC FAILURE[/bold][/color]

• [bold]VERIFICATION REQUIRED:[/bold] You must be [bold]ABSOLUTELY CERTAIN[/bold] that the individual being placed in the Oubliette carries a verifiable anomalous contaminant. The device's detection systems are calibrated specifically for these threats, not for standard punishment, detention, or discipline.

• [bold]EXPLOSION HAZARD:[/bold] Placing an individual [bold]without[/bold] an anomalous contaminant triggers a critical containment failure. The Oubliette will destabilize and detonate, causing:
  - Severe injury or death to the occupant
  - Significant structural damage to surrounding areas
  - Potential injury or death to nearby personnel
  - Loss of corporate assets

[head=3]EMERGENCY INTERVENTION PROCEDURE[/head]
[color=orange][bold]IF AN INDIVIDUAL HAS BEEN MISTAKENLY PLACED IN THE OUBLIETTE:[/bold][/color]

• [bold]IMMEDIATELY[/bold] locate a [bold]crowbar[/bold] or similar prying tool
• Apply crowbar to the Oubliette's access panel or door mechanism
• The device's emergency release is designed to halt the decontamination cycle when forcibly opened
• Extract the individual and assess for any partial contamination effects
• Report the incident to Medical staff immediately

[color=red][bold]DO NOT DELAY[/bold][/color] - The decontamination cycle progresses rapidly. Quick intervention with proper tools can prevent catastrophic failure and save the individual from severe harm.


• [bold]PERSONNEL PROHIBITION:[/bold] Under [bold]NO CIRCUMSTANCES[/bold] are personnel to voluntarily enter the Oubliette unless:
  - Directed by a qualified Science or Medical professional
  - Confirmed exposure to an anomalous contaminant has occurred
  
  Inserting an individual who does not have anomalous contaminants may be classified as:
  - Endangerment
  - Waste of corporate resources
  - Potential endangerment of others if detonation occurs

[head=3]PROPER USAGE PROCEDURE[/head]
1. Confirm anomalous contamination
2. Clear the immediate area of non-essential personnel
3. Place subject in Oubliette and activate per standard protocol
4. Monitor decontamination cycle until completion
5. Ensure outcome is handled appropriately
