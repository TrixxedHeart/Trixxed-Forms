[head=2]OBJECTIVE[/head]
Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

[head=2]CARD VALUES[/head]
• Number cards = pip value (2-10)
• Face Cards (Jack/Queen/King) = 10
• Ace = 1 or 11 (your choice, as long as it doesn't bust)

[head=2]HOW TO PLAY[/head]
1. Place your bet.
2. You get 2 cards face up.
3. Dealer gets 1 up, 1 down.
4. Choose:
   • [bold]HIT[/bold] = Take another card
   • [bold]STAND[/bold] = Keep your hand
5. Dealer reveals hidden card.
6. Dealer must hit until 17 or higher.
7. Closest to 21 wins.

[head=2]WINNING[/head]
• Have a higher total than the dealer without going over 21 = Win even money 1x
• Get a Blackjack (Ace + 10/J/Q/K on your first two cards) = Win 1.5x
• Tie with the dealer = Push (you keep your bet)
• You go over 21 = Bust and lose your bet
• Dealer goes over 21 = All remaining players win
