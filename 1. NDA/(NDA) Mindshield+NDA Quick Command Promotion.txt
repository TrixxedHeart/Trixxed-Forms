[head=3]ARTICLE II: NON-DISCLOSURE & CONFIDENTIALITY[/head]

[bold]AGREEMENT TYPE:[/bold]
■ UNILATERAL - 1st Party discloses, 2nd Party protects

[bold]CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION:[/bold]
■ All proprietary Nanotrasen research and technology
■ Command protocols and emergency procedures
■ Security systems, access codes, and strategic assets
■ Information marked "Confidential" or "Classified"
■ Corporate Secrets as defined by Nanotrasen Legal

[bold]DURATION:[/bold]
Obligations remain in effect until:
■ Information is no longer proprietary
■ Written release is provided by Nanotrasen Legal
■ Station emergency protocol mandates disclosure (per Article V)

[bold]OBLIGATIONS:[/bold]
The Candidate hereby affirms a lifelong undertaking of confidentiality. The Candidate shall:
• Maintain confidential information in strict confidence per SOP §4
• Report suspected unauthorized disclosures immediately to Central Command staff or via fax per SOP §5
• Never divulge Corporate Secrets to unauthorized personnel
• Accept and assist in carrying out any penalties for breaches imposed by Central Command staff per SOP §7

[head=3]ARTICLE III: MINDSHIELD IMPLANT - MANDATORY REQUIREMENT[/head]

[bold]PURPOSE:[/bold]
The primary purpose of the MindShield Implant is to protect The Corporation's proprietary information, strategic assets, and command-level operational parameters from external psychic influence, telepathic interrogation, subversion, and memetic hazards.

[bold]ASSUMPTION OF RISK:[/bold]
The Candidate acknowledges being informed of potential risks associated with the Mindshield implantation procedure. While classified as [italic]"Minimally Invasive"[/italic] by Nanotrasen Medical Division, understood risks include, but are not limited to: cephalalgia (headache), transient nausea, and in <0.01% of cases, aversion to snakes. The Candidate voluntarily assumes all known and unknown risks.

[head=3]ARTICLE IV: CONFIDENTIALITY BREACH PROTOCOL[/head]

[bold]REPORTING REQUIREMENTS:[/bold]
In accordance with General Command SOP §5, the Candidate must report any confidentiality breaks as soon as reasonable to:
■ A member of Central Command staff on station, or
■ Central Command via fax

[bold]EMERGENCY DISCLOSURE:[/bold]
Per SOP §6, the Candidate may divulge Corporate Secrets in a [bold]dire, station-threatening emergency[/bold] where such disclosure is deemed absolutely necessary for the immediate preservation of Nanotrasen assets and human life, and only to the minimum extent required. Such disclosure must follow the direction of Central Command staff when available. Documentation and reporting to Central Command must occur within 15 minutes of the emergency's conclusion.

[bold]PENALTIES FOR BREACH:[/bold]
The Candidate understands that failure to report breaches, unauthorized disclosure, or slander/libel against Nanotrasen (per SOP §3) will result in penalties imposed by Central Command staff per SOP §7.
