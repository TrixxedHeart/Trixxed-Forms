# Usage

## Audits
Audits are for audits done by an IAA of the department. You should go around asking questions and checking if the department is up to SOP.

I usually send them to the Nanotrasen Representative if one is present along to the relevant department head

## Self-reflections
These are my psychological torture device primarily for command.

You should hand these to the relevant personnel to fill out how well they believe they have been following SOP.
Once they're finished I encourage doing your own personal audit on the same form or so of how well *you* believe they're actually doing but not necessary.

I usually send the finished ones to the relevant head and the NTR.
