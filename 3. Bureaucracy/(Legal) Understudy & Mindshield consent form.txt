I, [color=#1b67a5]NAME[/color], herein referred to as the [bold]"Understudy,"[/bold] in consideration for participation in the Head of Personnel Understudy Program (hereafter [bold]"The Program"[/bold]) with Nanotrasen, do hereby voluntarily execute this briefing acknowledgment.

[head=3]ARTICLE I: ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF PROGRAM SCOPE & MINDSHIELD NECESSITY[/head]
The Understudy acknowledges that this is a [italic]training and observation role[/italic] and does not constitute a full Command-level appointment. Final authority on all departmental matters remains with the supervising Head of Personnel or the acting Captain.

Due to the Understudy's potential exposure to sensitive personnel data, departmental budgets, and other proprietary information, a **Mindshield Implant** is a mandatory condition for participation in The Program. Its purpose is to protect The Corporation's confidential data from external psychic influence and subversion.

[head=3]ARTICLE II: EXPOSURE TO SENSITIVE INFORMATION & CONFIDENTIALITY[/head]
The Understudy acknowledges that during The Program, they may be exposed to sensitive, proprietary, or classified Nanotrasen information (hereafter [bold]"Protected Data"[/bold]). This may include, but is not limited to: personnel records, psych evaluations, supply requisition codes, and internal policy drafts.

The Understudy hereby agrees to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all Protected Data. They shall not discuss, disseminate, or record this information outside of their official duties as directly supervised by the Head of Personnel. This confidentiality undertaking survives the termination of The Program.

[head=3]ARTICLE III: ASSUMPTION OF RISK & WAIVER OF LIABILITY[/head]
The Understudy acknowledges the standard risks associated with the Mindshield implantation procedure. While classified as "Minimally Invasive," understood risks include cephalalgia (headache) and transient nausea. The Understudy voluntarily assumes these risks and releases Nanotrasen from liability for standard procedural outcomes.

[head=3]ARTICLE IV: ACKNOWLEDGEMENT OF CONSEQUENCES[/head]
The Understudy understands that any attempt to remove, tamper with, or circumvent the Mindshield Implant, or any negligent/willful violation of confidentiality, will result in [color=Red]immediate removal from The Program, reassignment to a non-sensitive role, and may be subject to further disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal proceedings.[/color]
