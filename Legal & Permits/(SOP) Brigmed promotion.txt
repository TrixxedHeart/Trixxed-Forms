        [head=2][bold]STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
        BRIGMEDIC PROTOCOL[/bold][/head]
──────────────────────────────────────────

[head=3]I. PURPOSE & SCOPE[/head]
This document outlines the duties, responsibilities, and operational limitations of Medical personnel assigned to the Brigmedic (Brigmed) role. This assignment serves as a force multiplier for Security operations by providing dedicated medical support, and is [bold]not[/bold] a Security deputization.

[head=3]II. STANDARD OPERATING PROTOCOL (Condition [color=LimeGreen]Green[/color]/[color=DarkOrange]Yellow[/color])[/head]
[bold]A. Personnel & Deployment[/bold]
■ Personnel for this role [bold]must[/bold] be drawn from the Medical Department unless authorized otherwise.
■ The Brigmedic is a [bold]non-combatant[/bold] role operating under medical authority.

[bold]B. Primary Duties[/bold]
■ Maintain some presence in the Medical Bay, monitoring comms and crew monitor.
■ Be prepared to respond to the Brig to conduct health checks on prisoners or high-risk patients.
■ Ensure all individuals in Security custody receive appropriate medical care.

[bold]C. Limitations & Restrictions[/bold]
■ [bold]You are not a Security Officer.[/bold] You do not have arrest authority.
■ You may [bold]not[/bold] carry equipment of an offensive nature.
■ Permitted equipment is strictly limited to self-defense and medical tools.

[head=3]III. EMERGENCY PROTOCOL ([color=Red]Red[/color])[/head]
[bold]A. Deployment & Equipment[/bold]
■ Deploy to the forward edge of the active combat zone, behind Security lines.
■ Carry one (1) set of restraints and a small-arms weapon [bold]only[/bold] as a last-resort contingency.
■ Primary focus is rapid stabilization of Security personnel to return them to combat readiness.

[bold]B. Field Duties[/bold]
■ Administer immediate life-saving measures.
■ Retrieve and secure dangerous equipment from the fallen for later Security collection.
■ [bold]Priority One:[/bold] Avoid capture.

[head=3]IV. ROLE DIFFERENTIATION: BRIG PHYSICIAN vs. PARAMEDIC[/head]
[bold]Paramedic:[/bold] A reactive role. Responds to individual casualties, extracts them to Medical Bay, and focuses on patient transport and initial stabilization.
[bold]Brigmedic:[/bold] A proactive-support role. Embeds near the operational area to provide immediate, advanced care with the goal of keeping personnel in the fight.
